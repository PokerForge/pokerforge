PokerForge

Play · Analyse · Improve

A personal poker hand-history tracker and stats analyzer for Windows. Import your own hands, see your results over time, break your game down by position and street, and study your leaks — all on your own computer.

Free to try · No account required · Nothing ever leaves your computer

What it does

Overview

A cumulative results graph (Total / Showdown / Non-Showdown / EV, in $ or BB/100) with a draggable summary box.

Sessions

Every session grouped by date and stakes, drilling into a full hand grid and a hand-by-hand replayer.

Stats

Your own game broken down by position, an auto-generated "Your Leaks" section, and trends over time.

Population

A sortable villain pool with search/filter, and a full stat profile for any opponent you've played.

CSV export

Hand lists, By Position, Sessions, and the villain pool can all be exported for your own analysis.

Backup & restore

One-click backup and restore, plus multiple profiles for a shared computer or separate accounts.

Your data stays yours

PokerForge runs entirely on your own computer. There's no server, no account, and no analytics — the only network request it can ever make is a manual, one-off check you trigger yourself (Help > Check for Updates). Everything else, including your hand histories and stats, lives in a local database on your machine. See the Privacy Policy for details.
